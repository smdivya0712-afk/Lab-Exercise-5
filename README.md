Aim:

The UX Design Experiment aims to address critical user experience issues in two different digital design contexts:

Student Expense Management App Design: The goal is to develop a simple and user-friendly mobile application that helps students track daily expenses, manage budgets, categorize spending, and receive useful financial reminders. The app should reduce the difficulty of manually tracking expenses and help students develop better spending habits.

Online Appointment Booking Portal Redesign: The goal is to redesign a confusing appointment booking website used for scheduling appointments with doctors and service providers. The redesigned portal should focus on improving navigation, simplifying the booking process, clearly displaying available time slots, and reducing user confusion.

Algorithm:

Identify user pain points through interviews, surveys, and real-world observation. Define UX problem statements based on specific user needs.

Choose suitable research methods: Expense App: Interviews, Surveys, Usability Testing.

Appointment Portal: Contextual Inquiry, Heuristic Evaluation, Surveys.

Create user personas to represent typical users and their goals.

Use the collected insights to design improvements for usability, simplicity, and accessibility.

Output:

Student Expense Management App:

Identified key challenges such as difficulty tracking small expenses, lack of budgeting awareness, and forgetting payment dates. Designed features that:

• Allow users to record and categorize daily expenses. • Provide simple budget and spending summaries. • Send reminders for important payments and expenses. • Provide personalized spending suggestions.

Improved usability through a simple dashboard and easy navigation. Personas helped guide feature prioritization and user flows.

Online Appointment Booking Portal:
Discovered major usability gaps including:

• Difficult navigation and complicated booking steps. • Unclear availability of appointment slots. • Too many unnecessary form fields. • Confusing instructions and information.

Redesign included:

• Simplified appointment booking with fewer steps. • Clear display of available dates and time slots. • Reduced and organized form fields. • Improved navigation and readable instructions.

Personas ensured that the redesigned portal addressed the needs of different types of users.

General Outcome:
The research-driven approach provided a better understanding of user frustrations, goals, and expectations.

Implemented changes are expected to:

• Increase user satisfaction and engagement. • Reduce confusion and task abandonment. • Improve task completion and overall usability. • Encourage repeated use of both digital services.

User Problem Statements
Student Expense Management App
Problem Statement 1: Students need a simple way to track their daily expenses because manually recording spending is difficult, which results in poor budget management.

Problem Statement 2: Students need personalized spending information because they may not know where most of their money is being spent, which results in unnecessary expenses.

Online Appointment Booking Portal
Problem Statement 1: Users need a simple way to book appointments because the existing process contains too many steps, which results in confusion and booking abandonment.

Problem Statement 2: Users need clear information about available appointment slots because unclear schedules make selection difficult, which results in delays and frustration.

Research Methods

Expense App: Interviews, surveys, and usability testing were selected to understand students' spending habits and evaluate the ease of using the application.

Appointment Portal: Contextual inquiry, heuristic evaluation, and surveys were selected to identify navigation problems, usability issues, and difficulties faced during appointment booking.

User Personas
Persona 1 – Student

Name: Ananya Age: 20 Occupation: College Student

Goals: • Track daily expenses • Manage monthly budget • Reduce unnecessary spending

Behaviors: • Uses mobile payment apps frequently • Makes small daily purchases • Rarely records expenses manually

Pain Points: • Forgets to record expenses • Finds budgeting difficult • Does not know monthly spending patterns

Motivation: Wants to save money and manage her expenses independently.

Persona 2 – Appointment User

Name: Ravi Age: 45 Occupation: Working Professional

Goals: • Quickly find an available appointment • Book appointments without confusion • Receive clear booking confirmation

Behaviors: • Uses websites mainly when necessary • Prefers simple step-by-step processes • Checks appointment timings before booking

Pain Points: • Complicated forms • Difficulty finding available slots • Unclear instructions

Motivation: Wants to complete the booking quickly without needing assistance.
